# Gate 2: Meaning (reading, yes or no per item)

Only after Gate 1 passes. Per Strength and Weakness: sentence roles match S-E-E-R or S-Ex (sentence 1 a pattern; the example sentence one specific event or figure; the restate a restate, not a new theme or an instruction); the item describes a pattern, not a one-off, even though one example is shown ("Erich closed the largest deal of the year" is a one-off; "Erich closes large multi-site projects" is a pattern); one example (not two dated events, not a list of accounts or functions, not the self-appraisal quoted back); no instruction or note to self in any sentence. A "rather than" or "instead of" clause is an instruction only when its second half names what the direct should have done ("rather than by surfacing the concern through her manager"); a contrast in observed behavior ("rather than letting them sit") is fine. Weaknesses: repeat flag in the body when the theme was in the prior review. Core Message: Ramification fits the Result definition. Guidance: every Weakness covered; every point traces to a Weakness or the Ramification; every point has a 12-month measure.

Fix line form: "S3: sentence 2 is a list of five accounts; it must be one event or figure." "Core Message: the three areas named are W1 and W2; the Ramification must name the work of mine he takes on." No rewrites. The Bob examples carry the standard.

Work through every item, not only the ones that look wrong. Account for each Strength, each Weakness, the Core Message, and each Guidance point: it passes, or it carries a fix line. A sweep that stops at the first few problems costs a second Gate 2 round over items that were already in front of you.

Gate 2 email: same skeleton, "However, there are [N] meaning fixes needed before I review the content for consistency:", then the standards: 1 SEER (with Bob), 2 Sum-Ex (with Bob), 3 pattern not one-off, 4 one example per item, 5 repeat items say so, 6 Ramification and the four Result definitions, 7 Guidance traceability and measure. Drop what passed. Add a voice standard if a fix line needs one.
