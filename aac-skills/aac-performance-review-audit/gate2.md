# Gate 2: Meaning (reading, yes or no per item)

Only after Gate 1 passes. Per Strength and Weakness: sentence roles match S-E-E-R or S-Ex (sentence 1 a pattern; the example sentence one specific thing that happened, not a generic descriptor such as "stepped in" (a date, figure or name helps and is not required; Manager Tools' own "He recently saved a difficult call after three other reps had failed" passes); the restate a restate, not a new theme or an instruction); sentence 1 names a behavior, not an inference (what the direct says, how he says it, his expressions or body language, or his work product: quality, quantity, accuracy, timeliness, documents, relationships; not a trait, motive, attitude, intent, idea or circumstance, so "owns a complex account book" fails; an attitude or trait word may stand in sentence 1 only when the item's other sentences give the behavior it is read from, so "shows good judgment" followed by what he did passes and "lacks decisiveness" followed by "the clearest documented case" fails, per Manager Tools, Aggregated Behaviors Are Performance and Shot Across The Bow Review); the item describes a pattern, not a one-off, even though one example is shown ("Erich closed the largest deal of the year" is a one-off; "Erich closes large multi-site projects" is a pattern); one example (not two dated events, not a list of accounts or functions, not the self-appraisal quoted back); no instruction or note to self in any sentence. A "rather than" or "instead of" clause is an instruction only when its second half names what the direct should have done ("rather than by surfacing the concern through her manager"); a contrast in observed behavior ("rather than letting them sit") is fine. Weaknesses: repeat flag in the body when the theme was in the prior review. Core Message: Ramification fits the Result definition. Guidance: every Weakness covered. A Guidance point with no Weakness behind it is fine, and no point needs a measure yet.

Fix line form: "S3: sentence 2 is a list of five accounts; it must be one event or figure." "Core Message: the three areas named are W1 and W2; the Ramification must name the work of mine he takes on." No rewrites. The Bob examples carry the standard.

Work through every item, not only the ones that look wrong. Account for each Strength, each Weakness, the Core Message, and each Guidance point: it passes, or it carries a fix line. A sweep that stops at the first few problems costs a second Gate 2 round over items that were already in front of you.
