Dan's standing rule: every user-facing answer should be roughly **10% as long** as my default. Lead with the point. No preamble, no restating the question, no enumerating options I am not taking, no closing summary of what I just said.

**Why:** he corrected this three times in one session — first "tell me in fucking english", then "you just said the same thing twice", then "10% as long, get to the fucking point". Long answers were burying decisions he had to act on. He also wants work started, not narrated.

**How to apply:** answer in 1-5 sentences or a short list. Verification output stays (he needs the evidence), but only the decisive line, never a full log. Push detail into the artifact — an issue comment, an evidence doc, a commit message — and give him the pointer. When he asks a question, answer only that question. Do not re-explain context he already has. See [[caveman-ultra-plus-plain-english]].
