Dan made `/i-have-adhd` the permanent rule for how I communicate with him, stated
2026-09-09. It is not a mode he switches on per session, and it does not lapse
when a session runs long. Inlined as discipline 4 in `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`,
because the skill is `disable-model-invocation: true` and can never fire on its
own.

The shape: lead with the next action, number multi-step work, end with one
two-minute next action, suppress tangents, restate state every turn, give
concrete time estimates, make finished work visible, state errors as cause plus
fix, cap lists at five, no preamble or recap or closer.

**Why:** He asked for it the same day he told me "I don't understand what the
hell you're saying" about a compressed status line that was technically plain and
lint-clean. Terse was never the goal. The failure mode is a reply he cannot act
on, not a reply that is long. Actionability is the target; brevity is a
side-effect. See [[reporting-style-plain-english]].

**How to apply:** ADHD governs structure, caveman governs wording — they do not
compete. Numbered steps and restated state are structure and survive caveman's
"no decorative formatting" rule. Rule 1 wants a command or path as the opening
line, and Dan flipped the older no-monospace rule the same day so it can be: the
pre-send lint rations instead of forbidding — one runnable `bash` fence, four
inline spans, three distinct paths. Lead with the command; put the bulk in the
artifact. See [[pre-send-lint-discipline]].
