- Scope/design note for reviewers: profile/claude/settings.json's committed caveman hook entries (proxy .exe, shrink-hook, --adapter path under a specific nvm node version) are a hand-frozen snapshot from whichever machine last ran `caveman enable claude`; they are not portable to a fresh machine as-is. This change makes the new tools/caveman-desktop-install.ps1 the sole authority for that wiring after a pull: it lets the real @caveman-ai/cli CLI's own `enable claude` rewrite those paths for wherever it actually installed on the new machine, and strips them entirely (fail-closed) whenever install/enable did not succeed, rather than trusting the committed literal paths. This is the most direct reading of 'installs the pinned caveman CLI and runs caveman enable, failing closed'; an alternative reading (hand-rewriting the committed hook JSON in PowerShell without shelling to the real CLI) was rejected as far more fragile and out of scope for this ticket.
