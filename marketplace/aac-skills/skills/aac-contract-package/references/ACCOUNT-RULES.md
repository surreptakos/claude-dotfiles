# Account-Specific Rules — ACCOUNT-RULES.md

## Document control
- **Document type:** Standing exceptions by customer account
- **Audience:** Contract reviewers, Sales Admin, GPT-assisted reviewers
- **Purpose:** Hold the deliberate departures from the standard rules for specific accounts, so a reviewer stops re-flagging a settled decision every time a package for that customer comes through.
- **Created:** 2026-08-10

## How to use this file
Read this before reviewing any package. If the customer appears below, the entries here override BASELINES.md, the Schedule-to-Master Mapping Appendix, and the Living Standard for the items listed, and only for those items. Everything not listed follows the standard.

An entry belongs here when the departure is deliberate and recurring. A one-off decision on a single job stays on that job.

Do not raise an item covered by this file as a review finding or a Sales question.

---

## Chicago Park District (CPD)

**No master agreement.**
CPD does not sign our master agreement. Nothing in a CPD package refers to one. That means:
- The schedule carries no reference to a master agreement anywhere, including the validity clarification. Use "Pricing is valid for 30 days; pricing is based on all work being performed Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM." and stop there. Do not append "Repair Service hours are governed by the master agreement."
- No master agreement selections, no ¶4 recurring boxes, no billing-frequency checkbox, no rider.
- Do not ask which master governs, do not check that scheduled services map to master selections, and do not cite master paragraphs as authority in a CPD review.
- Where a standard clarification exists only to avoid duplicating a master provision, judge it on its own merits instead.
- The permit position still holds: Active Alarm files for and procures the permit, Subscriber pays the fees. That is long-standing practice, not a master term.

**Covered Equipment and Covered Sites addenda — not used.**
Do not fill, attach, or ask about the Covered Equipment - Security, Covered Equipment - Fire, or Covered Sites tabs on CPD packages, including packages that sell Repair Service. Hide those tabs before export.

**Alta Cloud Video with Analytics and 30 Days of Cloud Storage — $25.00 per camera.**
The Mapping Appendix lists $15 per camera as the standard price. CPD pays $25. Do not correct a CPD schedule down to $15 and do not raise it as a mapping mismatch.

**Repair Service — priced outside the standard formula.**
CPD Repair Service pricing is unique to the account and does not follow the standard derivation (`ESTIMATING-APPENDIX.md` §2) or the §3a FSI validation. Take the figure the estimator put on the schedule. Do not recalculate it, do not ask for an FSI worksheet, and do not raise the absence of one.

## Clearbrook (Clearbrook Center)

**Tax-exempt.**
Clearbrook is a tax-exempt nonprofit. Every Clearbrook schedule carries the tax-exemption clarification ("Pricing assumes Subscriber is tax-exempt and will provide a tax exemption certificate prior to purchase of equipment."). Do not price tax on a Clearbrook job and do not raise the exemption as a question. Added 2026-08-13 per Dan.

**Repair Service on new systems and full replacements** carries unique costs. Review against the approved pricing source rather than the standard rule. Confirm the current source before relying on it.

## Adding an account

When leadership settles a standing exception for a customer, add it here with:
- the account name as it appears on the schedule,
- what the standard says,
- what this account does instead,
- and a one-line reason if the reason is not obvious.

Keep entries short. This file exists to stop repeat questions, not to document the account.
