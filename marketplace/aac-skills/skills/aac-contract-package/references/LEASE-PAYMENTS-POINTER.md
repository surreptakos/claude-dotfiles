# Lease payments — pointer

**Status:** Active pointer, issued 2026-08-24. Executes the ruling on OPEN-DECISIONS item 18 (Dan, 2026-08-21) — the Lease Guide and the Lease Calculator no longer govern financed-deal payment quotes.

## Where financed-deal payment guidance lives

Financed installations run through LEAF. The lender issues the lease form and quotes the customer payment through its own quoting tool. Process, lender facts, and the drafter's steps are in `SOP-LEAF-Financed-Installations.docx` (v1.0, issued 2026-08-20). No in-house payment math is authoritative.

Anywhere a drafter would previously reach for AAC-as-lessor rate factors — the Lease Guide or the Lease Calculator — they route to the LEAF SOP instead.

## Superseded artifacts
